## 4. How it works (4 steps)

**English**

## Up and running in one afternoon.

1. **Create your shop.**
   Open `[BOT]` in Telegram, name your shop and connect your own bot from
   @BotFather. About 5 minutes.
2. **Connect your staff group and channel.**
   Add your bot and send one code. Group admins become owners, members
   become staff. No passwords to share.
3. **Add your products.**
   Photo, price and stock, by color and size, or just one number for bags,
   belts and jewelry. Post to your channel with one tap.
4. **Customers order.**
   They tap **🛒 Order** on a post, choose, and confirm. The order arrives in
   your staff group, and when stock runs out the post shows **SOLD OUT** by
   itself.

## 5. Features

**English**

## Everything a Telegram shop needs. Nothing it doesn't.

### For your customers
- **Order in Amharic or English.** The bot speaks their language, with buttons
  instead of typing.
- **Only what's really in stock.** Sold-out sizes are never offered, and items
  are held for 5 minutes while they order.
- **Pickup or delivery.** Delivery areas and fees are shown; delivery is paid
  when the items arrive.
- **Pay the way they already do.** Telebirr, CBE or any account you list.

### For your staff
- **Orders land in your staff group.** Everything the customer chose, their
  name and phone, ready to confirm.
- **Confirm payments in one tap.** Customers send the screenshot; staff check
  the money arrived and confirm.
- **Sell in the shop too.** Walk-in customers are recorded from the phone: the
  stock goes down and the channel post updates.
- **Discounts with limits.** Staff can lower the price only as far as you
  allow. The listed price never changes.

### For you, the owner
- **A dashboard inside Telegram.** Products, stock, orders and settings, on
  your phone. Nothing to install.
- **Know your numbers.** Sales today, this week, this month; Telegram vs in
  the shop; discounts given, by staff member; best-selling products.
- **Stock that keeps itself right.** Every order and shop sale updates the
  stock and the channel post. Low stock is flagged.
- **Your shop, your bot.** Customers talk to your shop's own bot, with your
  name.

### Smart, but you stay in control
The AI only helps the bot understand messages written in the customer's own
words. **Prices, stock and payments always come from you.** When the bot isn't
sure, or a customer wants to bargain or complain, it hands the chat to your
staff.
